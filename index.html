Do these now — it takes ten minutes

Presales are won weeks before they're announced. This is the whole trick:

  • Make sure your Ticketmaster account is set up with your phone verified and payment saved — and tap the ♥ on Justin's page so official alerts come to you.
  • Join the mailing list at justinbiebermusic.com — artist presale codes usually land there first.
  • Hit “Track” on Songkick and follow him on Spotify with notifications on — two more alarms that cost nothing.
  • Tell the crew to do the same. Every account registered for presale is another lottery ticket for good seats.

The day it drops

When the green lamp lights, we run this — in order, no improvising:

  1. Verify it's real. The announcement must be on Ticketmaster or the official site — not a fan account, not a resale site.
  2. Register for the artist presale the same hour. Registration usually closes days before the sale. Everyone in the crew registers.
  3. Sale morning: one device per person, signed in early, payment already saved. We coordinate in the group chat and go for seats together.
  4. Official channels only. Face value or verified — we're going to see him, not to feed scalpers. If presale misses, general sale is days later; patience beats panic-buying.
